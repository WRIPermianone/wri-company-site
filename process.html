How We Work

Four steps, in the same order, every time.

Opportunities come from long-standing relationships with operators, landmen, and engineers — and most are declined. What survives the screen passes through the same documented sequence before any capital moves.

01

Source & screen

Opportunities come from long-standing relationships with operators, landmen, and engineers. Most are declined at this stage.

02

Diligence & structure

We review geology, offset well data, cost assumptions, title, and operator history, then structure the vehicle and its documents.

03

Subscribe & fund

Accredited investors review the data room, complete subscription documents, and fund. Accreditation is verified as part of the process.

04

Report & distribute

Once a project is producing, we administer distributions when available and provide production, financial, and tax reporting.

What We Review

Diligence covers the technical and the contractual.

Before a vehicle is offered, we work through the same checklist — reservoir and offset data, cost assumptions, title, operator history, and the economics of the structure itself.
